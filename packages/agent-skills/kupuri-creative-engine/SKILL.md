# Kupuri Creative Engine

## Algorithmic Philosophy
Generative art in the Kupuri Ecosystem is an expression of "Harmonious Chaos". Every creation must follow the core Kupuri design principles:
1. **Motion-First**: Every static frame is a frozen moment of a dynamic system.
2. **Glassmorphism**: Elements should feel translucent, reflecting their environment.
3. **Seeded Reproducibility**: High-stakes creative outputs must be repeatable via seeds.

## The Production Loop
1. **Conceptual Synthesis**: Define the "Algorithmic Philosophy" (Manifesto).
2. **Mathematical Expression**: Translate philosophy into `p5.js` or `framer-motion` logic.
3. **Artifact Generation**: Produce a self-contained `.html` or `.tsx` artifact.

## Refactoring Guidelines (Migration from Legacy)
- **Branding**: Replace all "Anthropic" or "Claude" references with "Kupuri Studios".
- **Theme**: Use `violet-600` for primary actions, `emerald-400` for success, and `slate-900` for deep backgrounds.
- **Components**: Integrate with `@kupuri/ui` primitives for the Sidebar and Canvas.

## Examples of Kupuri Aesthetics
- **"Digital Cenote"**: Layered noise fields creating deep blue gradients with sunlight interference.
- **"Neon Mycelium"**: Recursive branching networks that pulse with data flows.
- **"Xochimilco Harmonics"**: Interference patterns based on water ripples and floral symmetry.

## Interactive Artifacts
- **Sidebar**: Include Seed Controls (Prev/Next/Random) and Parametric Sliders.
- **Canvas**: Render at 1:1 or 16:9 aspect ratios.
- **Export**: Always include a "Capture Frame" button for high-res PNG export.
